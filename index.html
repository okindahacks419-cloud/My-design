Manifesto Highlights

Our 4 Core Campaign Pillars

A clear, practical roadmap designed to deliver immediate improvements and long-term prosperity across Muhoroni Koru Ward.

Good Roads

Upgrading feeder roads, tarmac access routes, and drainage systems to ensure smooth transport for agricultural produce and easy ward connectivity year-round.

Pillar #1 • Infrastructure

Better Education

Expanding Ward Bursary allocations, supporting ECDE infrastructure, modernizing vocational training centers, and ensuring needy students stay in school.

Pillar #2 • Empowerment

Agribusiness

Empowering sugarcane growers, dairy farmers, and smallholders with subsidized inputs, value-addition facilities, and direct market access.

Pillar #3 • Economy

Youth Opportunities

Establishing youth innovation funds, sports tournaments, talent mentorship programs, and vocational equipment grants for young entrepreneurs.

Pillar #4 • Future
